Full buildThe Treehouse, near Fowey

A timber-frame cabin built amongst the trees, foundations to furniture.

This one is ours, designed and built by Thoughtful from start to finish. The plot is a steep wooded bank, so the first job was holding it back: tiered gabion walls, drainage and a sewage treatment plant, all in before any timber arrived.

The cabin stands on concrete pier foundations, with a timber frame, insulated walls and roof, a metal roof and timber cladding. Folding glazed doors open the living space onto a deck among the trees. The interior is finished; the deck and exterior are being completed now.

Inside, everything was made for the building: a birch plywood kitchen and cabinetry from our own workshop, a sleeping loft and ladder, a wood burner on a stone hearth, and a bathroom with a sunken microcement bath.

  • Groundworks, foundations and drainage
  • Timber frame, roof and cladding
  • Birch ply kitchen and cabinetry
  • Sunken microcement bath, hand-carved stone basin

Related work

Original floors, Bath, 2025

A Georgian wooden floor lifted, repaired and brought back.

Georgian boards were cut wide and to no standard size, so nothing off the shelf today will sit alongside them. The hard part of this job was sourcing boards of the same width to replace the damaged lengths. We found them in timber salvaged from a manor house that was being stripped out. With those in hand, the floor was relaid tight and sanded ready for finishing.
