A timber outbuilding made into a room for every season.

Rigid insulation to walls and roof, taped vapour layer, ply lining, a new floor and lighting.

Original floors, Bath, 2025

A Georgian wooden floor lifted, repaired and brought back.

Georgian boards were cut wide and to no standard size, so nothing off the shelf today will sit alongside them. The hard part of this job was sourcing boards of the same width to replace the damaged lengths. We found them in timber salvaged from a manor house that was being stripped out. With those in hand, the floor was relaid tight and sanded ready for finishing.

About Thoughtful

A building firm that designs, builds and finishes its own work.

Thoughtful is an independent building firm on the Fowey estuary, with nearly ten years of building behind it. The same team that pours the foundations makes the kitchen, so nothing gets lost between trades.

We take on a limited number of projects each year, from a single bathroom to a complete new building, so that each one is built and finished properly.

  • Design and buildWe draw up straightforward projects ourselves, and work in partnership with architects and engineers on complex ones.
  • Listed and older buildingsWe have worked on listed buildings. Changes that affect a listed building's character need consent before work starts, and we plan for that with you from the first visit.
  • Our own workshopKitchens, cabinetry, ladders and furniture are made by us, to fit the building.
  • Registered tradesElectrics and gas are done by registered trades, and you get their certificates.
  • InsuredWe carry public liability insurance.
  • Registered waste carrierRegistered with the Environment Agency, number CBDL666564.
